# Task lists

Operate mode. A personal planner used repeatedly on Android and desktop web. The primary action is capturing and completing a task in the correct polyday. Todoist is the user's explicit visual and interaction authority; scheduling and history follow the approved product specification.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Familiar Todoist task lists organised around personal wake intervals.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white content, pale warm-grey navigation, restrained tomato accent, neutral sans typography, thin separators, compact rows and coloured priority circles.

STORY: Identify the current polyday, see unfinished work, capture a task, complete it and revisit its history.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop sidebar at left, centred task column with date and polyday title, compact interval selector and task rows. Mobile uses a top title, horizontal interval strip and bottom navigation; the add action stays reachable.

FORM: User-pinned Todoist canon; code-led implementation. Seed is inapplicable to the explicitly chosen reference. Signature interaction: quick entry stays open for repeated capture; completion updates the list and sync state immediately after durable storage.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
